<h2 id="functional-prototype">Functional Prototype</h2>

### Purpose
The purpose of our prototype was to test whether our mechanism could reliably actuate a compressed air canister to produce a controlled air stream capable of removing spotted lanternflies during harvesting.

### What was Tested
We tested three main aspects:
whether the mechanism could apply sufficient force (25–45 N) to activate the trigger
whether the housing and components remained stable during operation
whether the air stream could effectively displace targets at a distance

### Outcome
Our prototype successfully demonstrated that the mechanism could actuate the trigger and generate a focused air stream. However, testing revealed limitations in structural stiffness and range, indicating the need for improvements in shaft strength and nozzle orientation to achieve our target performance.
